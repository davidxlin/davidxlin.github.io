curriculum vitae
I am a first-year Ph.D. student in computer science at The University of Texas at Austin. I am fortunate to be advised by Noah Golowich.

Previously, I was an M.S. student in computer science at Cornell University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Sid Banerjee and Éva Tardos. I was also an undergraduate at Cornell, double majoring in mathematics and computer science. During that time, I was fortunate to work with David Shmoys.
